About me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Earth System Science at Stanford University, working with Prof. Rob Jackson. I received my Ph.D. in Environmental Science and Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2025, advised by Prof. Ye Wu.

I combine multi-platform monitoring with data-driven models to quantify air pollution dynamics and human exposure in homes and cities. My goal is to inform building electrification strategies by evaluating their exposure and health benefits. At Stanford, I lead the Ultrafine Particle (UFP) project in the Electrification for Health program, measuring size-resolved UFP emissions from gas appliances in 60+ San Francisco Bay Area homes.

Outside research, I am a competitive distance runner and part-time coach. I placed 6th overall in the 2026 San Francisco Marathon.
